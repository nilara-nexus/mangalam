“Mangala Azhaippu” means an auspicious invitation.

Our story

The name மங்கல அழைப்பு joins mangalam (auspicious) and azhaippu (invitation). Since 2015 our designers have turned kolam, temple gopuram, peacock and jasmine into invitations that feel like your own family tradition.

We design for Hindu, Christian and Muslim weddings, as well as modern couples who prefer clean, minimal cards.
